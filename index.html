How it works

From a bare room to a priced estimate in three steps

01 · Plan

Scan the room or draw it

Either way you get an editable plan with real dimensions.

  • LiDAR scanning. Walls, windows, doors and furniture appear on the plan by themselves. Several rooms scanned in a row line up on one plan.
  • Drawing by hand. Place corners and type wall lengths. Snapping to right angles and existing points keeps the plan straight.
  • Edit anything. Move walls, shift doors and windows, add furniture, merge rooms, add floors with their own ceiling height.
02 · Work

Assign work to the room or one surface

Quantities come from the plan, so you never type an area by hand.

  • Walls, floor or ceiling. Add a job to the whole room or to a single wall.
  • Height bands. Tile up to 1.5 m and paint above it. Each job counts only its own zone.
  • Openings subtracted. Windows and doors come out of the wall area. Reveals and skirting are counted on their own, and a door between rooms counts once.
  • Your corrections. Override any quantity or add a percentage for cutting waste.
03 · Estimate

See every line: quantity × price

Set your prices once in the catalog. They fill every new project, and you can change them in any project.

  • Totals by room, floor and home. The estimate updates with every change to the plan or prices.
  • Lock the prices. Edits to your catalog will not change an estimate you already agreed on.
  • Compare with a quote. Put your quantities next to the contractor's and ask about the difference.

Questions

Do I need an iPhone with LiDAR?

No. Scanning uses Apple RoomPlan and needs a LiDAR sensor: iPhone 12 Pro or later, iPad Pro (2020) or later. On any other iPhone or iPad you draw the plan by hand, and every other feature works the same.

What is free and what costs money?

Plans, catalogs, measurements and the estimate on screen are free. Exporting an estimate as a PDF is a one-time in-app purchase for that estimate.

What happens if I change an estimate after paying?

The PDF you paid for stays in the export history and can be sent again for free. A changed project is a new export and needs a new purchase. The app warns you before you pay, and you can return the project to the paid version.

How accurate are the numbers?

Quantities follow your plan, and prices follow your catalog. Check key walls with a tape measure before ordering materials. The estimate is a guide, not a binding quote.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your own iCloud account. We do not run servers that receive your projects. See the Privacy Policy.
