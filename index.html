Questions

Can I use RoomCost without LiDAR?

You can draw by hand on any supported iPhone or iPad. Scanning rooms with Apple RoomPlan requires a LiDAR-equipped device.

Where do the prices come from?

You enter rates for work and materials in your catalog. RoomCost uses the dimensions of your plan to calculate quantities. Check key measurements before ordering.

What happens when export access ends?

Your projects and exported PDFs stay. You can share a saved PDF from export history or restore the project to that version. A new pass adds time after any access you already have.

Where are my projects stored?

Projects stay on your device. If iCloud is enabled for RoomCost, they sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple’s TestFlight app on your device, then open the RoomCost invitation. You can send feedback directly from TestFlight.
