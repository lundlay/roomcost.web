To help us answer faster, include your device model, the iOS version and the app version. For a scan or plan problem, a screenshot helps most.

Common questions

The scan button is missing

RoomCost requires iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 or later. The scan button appears on devices that support LiDAR room scanning. To draw by hand, tap a starting corner and enter the wall lengths.

The scan came out wrong

Scan in good light and move slowly along the walls. Every wall, door and window on the plan can be corrected after the scan.

Projects do not appear on my other device

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and turn on iCloud for RoomCost in iOS Settings. Syncing can take a few minutes.

How does export access work?

Planning and estimating are free. Your first PDF export is free too. A week, month or year of export access lets you create additional PDFs and export Excel and CSV files across all your projects. Access does not renew automatically. With a yearly pass, you can add your logo to each PDF page. When access ends, your projects stay on your device and you can still share saved PDFs from export history.

I paid but export did not open

Some payments are confirmed later, for example with Ask to Buy or a bank check. Export opens when the App Store confirms the payment. If it does not, write to us with the date of the purchase.

My access is missing on a new device

Sign in with the Apple Account you paid with. Open the PDF preview of a project, tap the export button and choose Restore purchases.

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the RoomCost beta invitation. Beta builds may have bugs. Send feedback from TestFlight or by email.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
