Common questions

The scan button is missing

Room scanning needs a LiDAR sensor, found on iPhone Pro and iPad Pro models. On other devices draw the plan by hand: tap a starting corner and type the wall lengths.

The scan came out wrong

Scan in good light and move slowly along the walls. Every wall, door and window on the plan can be corrected after the scan.

Projects do not appear on my other device

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and turn on iCloud for RoomCost in iOS Settings. Syncing can take a few minutes.

I paid but the PDF did not open

Some payments are confirmed later, for example with Ask to Buy or a bank check. The PDF unlocks when the App Store confirms the payment. If it does not, write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
